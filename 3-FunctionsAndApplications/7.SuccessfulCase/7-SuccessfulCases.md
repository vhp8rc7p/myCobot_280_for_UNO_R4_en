# Successful Cases

This topic is **independent of which controller board you use**. The arm
hardware, and therefore this information, is identical whether the base is
driven by an M5Stack Basic or an Arduino UNO R4.

Rather than restate it (and risk it drifting out of date), refer to the
official documentation:

**[Successful Cases — official myCobot 280 documentation](https://github.com/elephantrobotics/mycobot_docs/tree/main/myCobot_280_for_Arduino_en/3-FunctionsAndApplications/7.SuccessfulCase/7-SuccessfulCases.md)**

The demo projects are written against the M5 controller. Those that drive the arm purely over the serial protocol will work through the R4 transponder; those that use the M5 screen, buttons or speaker will not port without rework.
