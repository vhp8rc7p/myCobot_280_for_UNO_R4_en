# Product Parameters

This topic is **independent of which controller board you use**. The arm
hardware, and therefore this information, is identical whether the base is
driven by an M5Stack Basic or an Arduino UNO R4.

Rather than restate it (and risk it drifting out of date), refer to the
official documentation:

**[Product Parameters — official myCobot 280 documentation](https://github.com/elephantrobotics/mycobot_docs/tree/main/myCobot_280_for_Arduino_en/1-ProductInformation/2.ProductParameter/2-ProductParameters.md)**

Payload, reach, DOF, joint ranges and repeatability are properties of the arm, not the controller. See [5.3 Hardware Interface](../../3-FunctionsAndApplications/5.BasicFunction/5.3-HardwareInterface/RoboticArmElectricalInterface.md) for the electrical details that *do* change.
