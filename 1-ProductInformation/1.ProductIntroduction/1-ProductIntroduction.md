# Product Introduction

This topic is **independent of which controller board you use**. The arm
hardware, and therefore this information, is identical whether the base is
driven by an M5Stack Basic or an Arduino UNO R4.

Rather than restate it (and risk it drifting out of date), refer to the
official documentation:

**[Product Introduction — official myCobot 280 documentation](https://github.com/elephantrobotics/mycobot_docs/tree/main/myCobot_280_for_Arduino_en/1-ProductInformation/1.ProductIntroduction/1-ProductIntroduction.md)**

The only difference in an R4 build is the controller in the base. The arm, servos, Atom end-effector board and mechanics are unchanged.
